# Institutional Broadband Users
### Hospitals, Schools, Band/Government Offices

Institutional users are the buildings and organizations which host multitudes of people and practices, and provide 
essential services to the community; these can include hospitals, schools, and places of governance, for example.

These places are likely to require special consideration when building your broadband network. As high traffic locations which
require a much larger amount of broadband access and computing power when compared to your average home or business, it is
generally recommended that institutions be set up with a fibre connection for the sake of speed and long-term cost
reduction (despite being initially more expensive to deploy).

### SuperNet

In Alberta, over 4,200 institutions across 429 communities within the province are connected to fibre optic and wireless networks
via SuperNet, a large network connecting Calgary's internet exchange (YYCIX) to POP's across the province. There is a common
misconception that SuperNet provides broadband service across the province, and that nothing more needs to be done. This is untrue.
Depending on where you are in the province, you may have access to a POP which is connected to SuperNet, but the responsibility to provide
local access networks to that POP still lies with you and your community.

SuperNet was created to benefit large institutions like those described above, and was not intended for household or business
connections. When planning your community's network, it is important to research what access to SuperNet may look like
for you.
